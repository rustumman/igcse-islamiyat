Before you start reading

This course is built to be forgotten — on purpose.

Re-reading a paragraph until it feels familiar is the least effective way to learn it, even though it feels productive. Cognitive scientists Peter Brown, Henry Roediger and Mark McDaniel spent a decade testing what actually works, and published it as Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning. Five of their findings are built directly into every lesson here — delivered through scored, Khan Academy–style quizzes rather than passive reading:

01

Retrieval practice

Every few paragraphs you'll hit a Quick Check — a real multiple-choice question with instant feedback. Answering from memory, not re-reading, is what wires a fact in.

02

Generation

Lessons open with a question you can't yet answer. Guessing first — even wrongly — makes the correct answer, once you meet it, stick harder than if you'd just been told.

03

Interleaving

Each topic's Course Challenge mixes questions from every sub-topic in shuffled order, the way the real paper will, instead of drilling one topic in a block.

04

Spaced retrieval

Each topic's review planner schedules you to retest yourself on Day 1, 3, 7 and 16 — the intervals that convert short-term recall into long-term memory.

05

Scored mastery + XP

Every Quick Check and Unit Test earns XP toward your level. One question at a time, instant right/wrong feedback, and a final score that tells you honestly whether you've mastered it.

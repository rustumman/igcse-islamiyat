## LESSON: The Night in the Cave
Lesson slug: night-in-the-cave

### Lede
On one night in Ramadan, 610 CE, everything changed — understanding exactly what happened is worth as many marks as knowing what was revealed.

### Predict Box
Every earlier scripture Muslims believe in was handed down essentially complete. Why might the Qur'an have arrived differently? Write a guess before reading on.

### Content
By the age of forty, Muhammad (pbuh) had developed a habit of withdrawing to a small cave on Jabal al-Nur to reflect in solitude. It was there that the angel **Jibreel** appeared to him for the first time.

#### What Jibreel commanded
Jibreel issued a single command: "Iqra" — "Read." The Prophet, who could not read, replied each time that he could not — and each time Jibreel embraced him tightly before repeating the command.

### Pull Quote
> "Read in the name of your Lord who created — created man from a clinging substance."
> — Qur'an 96:1–2

### Timeline
- **Ramadan, 610 CE** — Jibreel commands "Iqra" — Three times, each followed by an embrace.
- **Same night** — He returns home trembling — Convinced something terrible had happened to him.
- **Days later** — Waraqah ibn Nawfal confirms it — Identifies the visitor and predicts what is coming.

### Exam Lab
Past paper reference: 0493/11 · Paper 1, Q2
Sessions seen: May/June 2022, May/June 2023, Nov 2025
Question: (a) Describe the Prophet's experience of receiving the first revelation. [10 marks]
AO1 model answer points:
1. Context: withdrawing to the Cave of Hira at age forty.
2. Jibreel commanded "Iqra" three times.
3. The Prophet returned home trembling.
Examiner note: Candidates who prepared only the "different ways revelation came" answer often reuse it here and lose marks for not telling the actual story.

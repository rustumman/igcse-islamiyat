The life and importance of the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh)

From a boy orphaned three times before the age of eight, to a shepherd and trader trusted by all of Makkah, to the night in the Cave of Hira, through years of persecution and the Hijrah, the battles and treaties that built the first Muslim state in Madinah, the qualities that defined his character, and one Arabic title — khatam an-nabiyyin — that closes the whole chain of prophecy. Six units, built around named, dated, source-checked detail.
